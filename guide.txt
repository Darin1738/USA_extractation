Research Topic: Lasting effects of slavery on the American criminal justice system

"Modern injustice is not slavery, but it is slavery's legacy in a new form."

Thesis: Slavery was abolished, but its logic of controlling and extracting from Black bodies was repeatedly rebuilt in new legal forms: Black Codes, convict leasing, Jim Crow, and now mass incarceration. Today's version is subtler because it is racially neutral on paper, but its effects are not neutral.
