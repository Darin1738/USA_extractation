## How each downloaded file must be logged
For every image placed into `assets/external/` or reused in the board, record in `assets/external/SOURCES.md`:
1. **Filename** (what we saved it as)
2. **Title** as given by the archive
3. **Creator/photographer** and date
4. **Institution & collection**
5. **Item URL** (permanent link / LCCN / NAID)
6. **Rights statement** verbatim (e.g. "No known restrictions on publication" / "CC0")
7. **Where used** (which panel or background layer)

## Constraints
- No known-rights images (e.g. Corbis; some AP/Getty) are **excluded**.
- Where a person's death or lynching photos are graphic, prefer **ruins, objects, documents and dignified portraits** over victim imagery — consistent with the board's existing choice to omit graphic lynching photographs.
- Every LoC/NARA/CC0 pick still needs its **exact item page URL** — a search-results page is not a citation.
