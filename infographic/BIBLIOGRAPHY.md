## Data, reports and journalism

18. **SlaveVoyages.org** — David Eltis et al., *Voyages: The Trans-Atlantic Slave Trade Database.* Map and pie chart on P2/P9; figures 12.5M embarked / ~10.7M disembarked alive in the Americas.
